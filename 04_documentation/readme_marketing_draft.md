# Whisper 🍃

*A calm companion for your mind.*

**Whisper** is a minimalist, highly aesthetic mobile application built with Flutter. Unlike traditional, high-pressure to-do lists, Whisper serves as a distraction-free space for passive reminders, daily intentions, and inspirational quotes.

## 🧘 Why Whisper?
- **Calm by Design:** Fluid micro-interactions, clean typography, and adaptive light/dark themes engineered to reduce cognitive load.
- **Passive Scheduling:** Set up flexible delivery schedules, like receiving "3 inspirational quotes a week," without the anxiety of a growing backlog.
- **Private & Local-First:** Your thoughts never leave your device. Powered by the ultra-fast, local Isar database.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Aesthetic UI:** Deep Obsidian Black and Slate White themes with minimal borders.
- **Smart Recurrence:** Choose between exact intervals (e.g., every Monday) or flexible frequencies (e.g., 5 times a month).
- **Frictionless Interactions:** Swipe-to-acknowledge gestures and haptic feedback.
